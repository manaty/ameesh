Patch version 1.4.1 (L48: fast turn failures).

**Defect** (seen when switching an agent to DeepSeek): a harness that could not be found, or that exited at once, was relaunched every 5 s with no end. 59 turns were counted in 5 minutes, with the instruction replayed each time.

**Fix** (runner):
- a turn that does not start, or that fails in less than `AMEESH_FAST_FAILURE_S` (60 s), is a fast failure;
- the wait before the next turn doubles with each consecutive fast failure, from 5 s up to `AMEESH_FAILURE_BACKOFF_MAX` (300 s);
- after `AMEESH_MAX_FAST_FAILURES` (5) consecutive fast failures, the agent is stopped:
  - `stop_reason` = `erreur`;
  - the instruction stays pending;
  - the stop is recorded in the thread, and the stopped-agent alert notifies the person in charge.

  `ameesh resume` restarts it once the cause is fixed.
- a successful turn resets the series; a slow failure does not count.

Docs: `EXPLOITATION.md` covers this behaviour, as well as `AMEESH_DSH_BIN` under systemd (the shortcut fails there). `site/docs/reference/configuration.md` documents the three settings.

Tests: `tests/test_l48_echecs_rapides.py`. Full suite green with both drivers (psql, psycopg): 1420 tests.
