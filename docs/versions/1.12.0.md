**Review and merge**
- Review-after-merge lots (#1280, #2377, #2374, #2385, #2384, #2402, migration 0435): a REVUE lot is taken only by the family opposed to the author at the freeze (#2377); `work close --done` ends a branchless review lot once its verdict is in, and an opinion superseded by its parent is rectified (#1280, migration 0435 adds `done` to `close_reason`; the verdict must prove the mission and its reader, and an opinion does not open the review of an opinion). The merge gate of such a lot is in the sensitive class and asks for two opinions (#2374): two opinions without a proven author family are refused, a current blocker refuses the closure and the first mark prevails, and the link to the review lot created is preserved. The package correction of the review of ameesh-cx1 (#2402, B1) makes `work close --done` pass through `guard_delivery` too (a single `ok` on another sha, or a current `blocked`, used to close the mission as `done`). A pinned review-after-merge lot counts its reviews before delivery, closing the workflow-covered case (#2385), the closing instructions follow the guard and the review body explains the "author family not traced" route (#2384).
- Review gate follows the canon scale (#2394): `required_verdicts` is aligned with the canon (doubt or empty file list: one opinion, critical: two), a policy doubt keeps its paths for the gate and the independent family is kept for any doubtful class, also when no canon is readable.
- Reviewer designation at the freeze (#2447): the designation of reviewers is activated at the `frozen` milestone when the host reads a canon and the call owns its transaction (policy at the audited canon commit, frozen diff, exact provenance, real families of the registry), the thread is released after COMMIT; `work milestone` says the designation, `work reviewers` re-reads it and `--apply` replays the same freeze (waiting for capacity or canon is explicit and notified to the pilot, manual designation stays the journaled exception). The reviewer designated is woken (#2478), and the transaction takes the reviewer's column and the resumption together (#2447 review cx2).
- A mechanical realignment on the target no longer asks for a new review (#2442, gate #1979): `verdict_carry` carries the verdict across a realignment that adds nothing, the exact date of a carried-over verdict is kept, writing is serialized and the structural proof of the realignment is checked (R1 cx1 + qa-01). A rebase that leaves aside a commit already in the target no longer loses the verdict (#2486: shallow clone, lease).
- Review proposals (#1738): a per-lot lock, the designated reviewer, a switch and the stack text; taking a proposal is revalidated under the lock, serialized with verdicts and freezes, and `work.claim_review_proposal` is classified (refused) in the executor contract.
- Fire and landing (#2256, #2370, #2349, #2389): the queue/direct lane comes from the project, not from the host; an implicit direct is refused and a residual auto-merge is disarmed outside the direct lane (#2256); the merge reconciliation covers an abbreviated freeze (#2370); `work fire` compares SHAs by prefix, a resolved side compares by its full SHA (an unknown head sharing the abbreviation does not cover it), and `--repo` is read as a folder or `owner/name` (#2349); the `no-land` of the fire is logged once per lot and fire sha instead of one LOUD line at each pass (#2389).
