## Finding

On 2026-10-10, `ameesh accounts list` showed the primary Claude account at 35 % of its 7-day window (pace 55 %), and the secondary and tertiary accounts never used: their weekly plans were being lost. Choosing by expiry (0034 §1, L74) always gave the primary, the only one with an open window, and session continuity froze the situation.

## Amendment to 0034 (owner's agreement), lot L117

For a new session or a rotation, ameesh picks, among the usable accounts, **the one furthest behind its pace**:

- lag = `used / min(90 %, elapsed share + 10 points)`, taken on the account's most constraining window;
- an account with no reading, or whose windows have expired, counts as 0 % and goes first;
- on a tie, the capacity that expires soonest (the original rule), then the declared order.

Unchanged: forcing, continuity below the threshold, the pause when all accounts are at the threshold, and Codex.

The reason for the choice appears in the runner's log, the switches and `ameesh accounts list`: "no reading: 0 % used, furthest behind its pace; ahead of: …".
