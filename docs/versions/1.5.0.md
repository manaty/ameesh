Version 1.5.0: seven lots integrated on `main` (9ee92a5). The full suite is green with both drivers (psql: 1514 tests OK, 6 skipped; psycopg: 1514 tests OK, 1 skipped).

## Lots

- **L60: cost and rotation of DeepSeek sessions.** DeepSeek turns now record their real session, usage and model: no more "unknown" model billed at the pro rate, which overestimated the cost about 12 times. A context ceiling (`context_max_tokens`, 15 M by default, adjustable per agent) rotates the session instead of only alerting. A turn already recorded is no longer written twice (migration 0041).
- **L61: fast commands despite a remote database.** psycopg becomes a dependency. Reads are grouped: 1 to 3 round trips per command instead of 4 to 49, and a test bounds that number. The hook is lighter, and `cost gauges` and `progress` no longer write. Against a remote database at 183 ms, `work list` goes from 19 s to 2.3 s and `cost report` from 83 s to 2.3 s.
- **L62: per-project view.** New `ameesh projects` command, PROJECT column in `ameesh list`, and the view at the top of `ameesh progress`.
- **L70: budget in the database for the whole mesh.** `ameesh budget`, `budget set` and `budget unset` manage hourly and daily caps, global or per agent. Runners reload them live, and each change is logged (migration 0042).
- **L71: accounting accuracy.**
  - The harness is read from the registry, which restores the Claude gauges.
  - `accounts list` and `cost report` are read-only.
  - The cost of a resumed session is counted as a difference.
  - The balance is read only once for the whole database.
  - A reading whose window has expired counts as 0 %.
- **L72: the runner survives a transient database outage.** It retries with a doubling wait, reconnects, and the current turn survives within the lease margin. The "database unreachable" status clears by itself, and the connection timeout goes to 10 s.
- **L74: decision 0034.** ameesh first uses the plan capacity that expires soonest (amends 0027). `accounts list` shows the capacity that will be lost at the next reset.
